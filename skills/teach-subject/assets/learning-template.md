# Learning artifact template

## learning/README.md

Inputs and source versions; links to plan, questions, exercises, progress, and separate tutor notes; completed work; exact pending prompt ID; next action; preparation time spent and remaining estimate. Keep tutor notes out of learner-facing summaries.

## learning/plan.md

Objective ID; observable behavior; prerequisite objective IDs; research links; original assessment evidence; estimated duration; validation task IDs; status. Order by dependencies. Account separately for lessons, practice, reassessment, and remaining research.

## learning/questions.md

Question ID; objective; prerequisites; learner-facing prompt; relevant source links. Build extensive coverage across explanation, prediction, counterexamples, and application, including APIs, invariants, tradeoffs, and failure modes. Present only one prompt at a time during tutoring.

## learning/exercises.md

Exercise ID; objective; prerequisites; estimated duration (normally 5–15 minutes); prompt; observable acceptance criteria; constraints preventing target-solution disclosure. Use small independent problems. No completed solutions.

## learning/tutor-notes.md

Prompt ID; expected reasoning dimensions; evaluation rubric; common misconception signals; progressive hints; transfer question; supporting research. Criteria may identify reasoning requirements but must not contain a completed target solution. Do not expose notes before the learner attempts a task.

## learning/progress.md

Objective and task ID; date; learner response or faithful summary; evaluation with evidence; hints used; demonstrated understanding; remaining gap; revisit task; status (untested / practicing / demonstrated / revisit). Preserve original assessment links.

Handoff: explainable mechanisms; defended tradeoffs; predicted failures; meaningful validation the learner can specify; untested areas; next design question.
