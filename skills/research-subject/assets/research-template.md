# Research artifact template

Adapt headings; replace prompts with evidence. Do not fill unknowns with guesses.

## research/README.md

- Problem summary and links to every artifact and concept page.
- Inputs and current stage; completed work and next action.
- Preparation time spent, remaining estimate, reason for revisions.
- Prioritized reading path: source/section, prerequisite, purpose, estimated time.
- Deeper references; readiness evidence and remaining gaps.
- Handoff: assessment status, unresolved questions, recommended first learning objective.

## research/assessment.md

For each assessed concept: question/task, learner response or faithful summary, observed reasoning, demonstrated knowledge, misconception and evidence, untested area, confidence and next probe. Include assessment date and changes since prior assessment.

## research/problem.md

Intended behavior; inputs/outputs; non-goals; workloads; constraints and resource limits; correctness criteria; performance goals and measurement conditions; prerequisites; glossary. Distinguish learner requirements from assumptions awaiting confirmation.

## research/concepts/<concept>.md

Prerequisites and links; mechanism; APIs and contracts; algorithm; data flow; ownership/lifecycle; invariants; failure modes; correctness and performance implications; counterexample; evidence labels and source IDs. Link diagram edges or captions to supporting sources. Explain applicability limits.

## research/sources.md

For each source ID: title, author/organization, original URL, publication date, version or commit, access date, relevant sections/lines, claims supported, applicability limits, availability, local paper/repository path if acquired. Record unknown dates explicitly. Repositories: origin and pinned commit. Papers: bibliographic identity and accessible version. Record unavailable sources without claiming to have read them.

## research/tradeoffs.md

Approach; objectives served; assumptions; advantages/costs; accepted wisdom and counterexamples; evidence; workload/version applicability; comparable state-of-the-art results as of a stated date. Separate measurements from predictions.

## research/unknowns.md

Question ID; conflicting or missing evidence; consequence; priority; proposed source/experiment; expected discriminating observation; status and resolution evidence.

## research/ai-assistance.md

Task-specific opportunity (for example mechanical bindings or refactoring); learning prerequisite; understanding risk; verification required; what the learner must retain ownership of. Recommendations only; no implementation authorization.
